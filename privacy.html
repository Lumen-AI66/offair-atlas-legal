Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Off-Air Atlas Engine (the "App") is a private tool used by one person, the owner of the YouTube channel Off-Air Atlas, to upload videos to that channel. The App is not offered to the public.

What the App accesses

The App asks the owner for one Google permission: uploading videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (youtube.upload). It cannot read, edit or delete existing videos, comments or account data.

What is stored

An OAuth token that proves the owner's consent is stored on the owner's own server. It is used only to upload videos to the owner's channel. The App does not collect, sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services and is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; deleting the stored token from the server removes the App's access as well.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the channel page: youtube.com/@OffAirAtlas.
